Andromeda, Mt Ngungun

Andromeda is a small crag with a lot of easy climbs suited for beginners. It was developed early in 2010. The climbs have been protected with glue-in carrot bolts so be sure to take your bolt plates.

There are still loose rock about, so take care not to pull rock off onto your belayer. Andromeda is shady most of the time and slow to dry after rain. Give it a few days of dry weather after heavy rainfall.

Use the tourist walking track system of Mt Ngungun. The track switches R and L up the slope. There are flights of steps along the track, go up 4 flights but walk past the fifth, approx 10m to a tree at the switch back. Leave the track here, walk around the tree and continue walking north-east for approx 10 minutes staying on the contour, following the faint path and a few green plastic strips tied to the trees. There are a few cairns along the way as well. You come to the black rock face of Andromeda.

As you walk along the rock face, find the biggest tree along the cliff approx. 2m away from the wall. There are climbs to the L and to the R of that tree. The climbs are initialed. Starting R of the tree:

Lunar Eclipse 17m 15
Climb straight up following the line of bolts. After the 3rd bolt traverse right to CSs anchor. 4 BRs.
Steve Jones, Ros Jackson, Heinz Buscher 23/05/2010

Coal Sack 17m 16
Start: 3m R of LE.
This climb follows the crack line. Place cams to protect the lower section of the climb. When the crack disappears clip the one BR up high. DBB is straight above.
Heinz Buscher 20/05/2010

Worm Hole 17m 18
Start: 4m R of CS just R of little tree.
Up the steep wall on small holds. After clipping the 2nd bolt trend L. From 3rd BR straight up then traverse L to CSs anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 20/05/2010

Southern Cross 20m 18
Start: 5m R of WH.
Climb following the line of bolts. Crux is between first and second BR. At the top, traverse R to IJ anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher, Sandra Buscher 20/05/2010

Space Ride 28m 16
A link-up. This is a traverse using the bolts of the various climbs. Start at SC, clip the first bolt then traverse R to clip Ijs first bolt, 1st bolt of GE is next, continue to Ses second bolt, on to Abs 2nd bolt, over to AEs 3rd BR straight up from here to anchor. Don’t forget to clip last BR of AE. 7 BRs.
Heinz Buscher, Gordon Baudino 18/09/2010

Interstellar Journey 20m 15
Start: 3m R of SC at the shallow crackline.
Follow the crackline to anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 20/05/2010

Gravitational Equilibrium 20m 18
Start: 2m R of IJ.
Climb up to high first bolt, clip and enjoy interesting moves up the cliff. Trend L after 2nd FH. Clip last FH and climb L to anchor. 1 BR, 3 FHs.
Heinz Buscher, Gordon Baudino, Glenn Ferguson 18/09/2010

Solar Eclipse 20m 16
Start 4m R of IJ and climb following the line of bolts. At the top traverse L to IJs anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher, Ros Jackson 23/05/2010

Asteroid Belt 18m 16
Start 3m R of SE and climb following the BRs. After clipping the last bolt traverse R to AEs anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 06/06/2010

Alien Encounter 18m 15
Start: Start 2m R of AB.
Follow the crackline to the anchor directly above. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 06/06/2010

Horse Head Nebula 17m 17
Start: Start 4m R of AE.
Move up the slabby ramp to the steep face. Up to first BR and onto ramp. Stay away from the loose blocks on your L. Clip FH from here and crank up. Clip next and last BR then trend L to AEs anchor. 2 BRs, 1 FH.
Heinz Buscher 06/06/2010

Starting from the tree to the L, the following climbs exist:

Event Horizon 17m 14
Climb up avoiding the loose blocks on your L then trending L up the steep wall. Traverse L to SDs DBB. 3 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 13/05/2010

Star Dust 17m 13
Climb straight up following the BRs keeping the large loose boulders on your R. A bit run out at the top but its easy climbing there. DBB. 3 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 13/05/2010

Nebula 10m 11
Straight up clipping the bolts then L to OTs DBB. 3 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 13/05/2010

Orbital Tether 10m 12
Start: Start at the OT mark.
Climb straight up to DBB. 3 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 09/05/2010

Black Hole 10m 16
Start: Straight under the overhanging block.
Climb up and over block. Once up, trend R to OTs DBB. BRs are a bit hidden in pockets. If you surmount the overhanging block more on the R of the bolt the grade changes to a 13. 3 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 09/05/2010

Orion's Belt 25m 13
Another link-up. Start as for Black Hole, clip the first bolt, traverse R to clip 2nd bolt of OT, keep traversing to 2nd bolt of N, 3rd bolt of ST and 3rd bolt of EH. Climb up trending L to anchor. An interesting but easy traverse.
Heinz Buscher, Sandra Buscher 23/05/2010

The next climbs are approx. 80m east. You come to a high slabby wall. As you walk along the rock face you come to a large corner with a black tree just half a metre away from the rock. SD starts here.

Space Dust 28m 10
Start: 10m R of SO at the tree with the black bark close to the rock face.
Climb up trending L all the way. After clipping the 4th bolt traverse L to anchor, clip last bolt on the way. This climb shares SOs anchor. Enjoy the view. 5 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 17/06/2010

Galaxy 26m 11
Start: Start 6m to the R of SO.
This route is run out at the start. Climb straight up and clip 1st BR approx halfway up the easy slab. As it gets steeper the bolts are placed closer. This climb trends slightly L and shares 4th BR with SDs 5th. Traverse to the L to DBB. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 03/07/2010

Synchronous Orbit 27m 16
Start below headwall. Climb slab clipping 2 BRs to the start of headwall. Clip 3rd BR and crank up the steep headwall until it eases off. Clip last BR and run up to DBB. A 50m rope will get you back down.
Heinz Buscher 03/07/2010

Singularity 26m 12
Start: 4m L of SO.
This climb follows a smooth looking part of the rock face. Climb up the easy slab following the bolts. As it gets steeper so does the grade. This is a climb that relies on good foot work and friction. After last bolt trend R to shared anchor. 4 BRs.
Heinz Buscher 03/07/2010
