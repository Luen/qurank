Just Juice (Mt Beerwah) route description

By Lee Skidmore, November 1999

Getting to the starting point
When you hit the Beerwah rock apron walking up the tourist track, walk about 200m (like 5-10 mins) left. It's on the right hand side of a smallish (15m wide and 5m deep) hollow. There is a boulder there right next to the rock. There is also a boulder further back from the rock that you can stand on to observe the route. Look up at the roofs and identify the black right facing corner. Get out the binoc's and look for three FH's on this corner. If you see 'em you're in the right place. 

Pitch 1 45m 15
Start right near the boulder and work up the twin seams to a bomber wire placement at about 4m. Another backs this up a little higher. This pitch absolutely eats large wires so take lots. Now relax and enjoy the slabby crackline that leads you right up to below the roof. If I remember rightly, it basically trends rightwards up to below the roof. The top half is more runout than the bottom half. In the last 15m I placed one piece. The belay is in a bowl (perfect bum sized depression) below the roof and consists on 3 bolts + chain (one mega bolt) in very soft rock. When the second gets here the belay becomes squishy.

Pitch 2 48m 19
First half of this is cool, second half kinda sucks. Slap top of roof and lean out around lip to clip the first FH. No problems. Turning the lip is the crux move and I couldn't do it at the time without dogging. Anyway, pull that move and get established in the corner and clip second FH. This corner is very short but extremely sustained climbing. Clip third FH and quite hard and taxing moves lead you out of the corner and onto a slab. Walk up this always trending left (as opposed what I did and ended up with no pro way out right on deathy removable holds). So up and up with shocking rope drag and very little pro with mega easy but loose climbing to the chain on the flat slab up and left of the corner. A very long pitch. Above the corner (30m of climbing) I had 3 pieces of gear in. Runout. Get comfortable on the slab (yeah right) and belay up the second.

Pitch 3 45m 13
Another long pitch that goes straight up the slab above the chain. Finishes in the 'toilet bowl' - a large bowl shaped dirt covered ledge with trees and shit. Take some tape for a rap off. This pitch is supposedly fun, but we didn't do it cause one of us became dehydrated. Rap the route to get off.

Overall
A big fun climb, with a technical second pitch, that will keep you amused for a number of hours on the sunny black rock that is Beerwah. Hail to the beer god.
