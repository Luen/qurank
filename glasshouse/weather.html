Weather

Climbing in the summer months (December to March) is possible and even enjoyable on most parts of the mountains as long as you get a very early start and climb while it is still shady. Don’t get trapped on the black slabs of Tibro and Beerwah in full summer sun as you’ll be liable to burst into flame! In addition to being hot, the summer months are also the wettest months of the year. The slabs at the base of Tibro and Beerwah can remain wet for days after heavy rain due to run-off from the upper sections. The winter months (June to August) like elsewhere in South East Queensland are a wonderful time to be climbing. Winter tends to produce long periods of stable, cool and dry conditions.
