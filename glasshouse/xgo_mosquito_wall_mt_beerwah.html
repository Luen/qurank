Mosquito Wall, Mt Beerwah

A small area on the way to Thanksgiving Wall - only a 10 minute walk from the car park. Up the tourist track to the start of the slab and seating area. Continue R from here, following the slab another 70m or so, till you come to a short vertical wall. A good summer crag as it is shaded all year in the morning and most of the afternoon. This is a pure spot climbing area, you will need bolt plates.

Low Irritant 12m 15
Start: Locate the line of bolts furthest L.
Three bolts to chains on R.
